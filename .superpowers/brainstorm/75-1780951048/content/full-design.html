Section Guide

① Header

Keeping existing header. No changes needed — already professional.

② Trust Bar

New thin dark bar: Free Shipping · 30-Day Returns · Secure Payment · 24/7 Support. Huge credibility signal.

③ Subnav

Quick category links. Polished with emojis and a "Today's Deals" hot link in orange.

④ Hero + Sidebar

Category sidebar added on the left (Amazon-style). Lists all categories with colour dots. Banner carousel on the right, unchanged logic.

⑤ Deal of the Day

Full-width dark banner. Shows one featured product with title, original/sale price, animated live countdown timer, and CTA button.

⑥ Flash Deals

Improved section header with orange left-bar. Countdown timer. Each product card now shows: star ratings, review count, strikethrough original price, and Add to Cart button.

⑦ Promo Banners

Existing banners polished: sharper text hierarchy, better CTA button styling, improved overlay gradients.

⑧ New Arrivals

Same grid, improved product cards. "New" badges, star ratings, and Add to Cart button now always visible (not just on hover).

⑨ Brand Strip

Brand logo row cleaned up with consistent sizing and subtle border/shadow treatment.

⑩ Newsletter

Orange gradient strip before footer. Email input + Subscribe button. Social proof copy ("Join 50,000+ subscribers").
